THE INNOVATION

Big Knowledge. Tiny Hardware.

An ultra-minimal approach to offline digital learning.

🔧

Ultra-Minimal Hardware

Built using only an ESP32 and a 256-GB microSD card, eliminating the need for a Linux-based computer or complex server setup.

💰

Approximately $10–11

Designed as an ultra-low-cost and low-power educational technology solution.

No Internet Required

🐧🚫

No Linux Required

No Linux computer, complex server, or software installation is needed for learners.

📶

Classroom Ready

Approximately 30 metres of Wi-Fi range and support for approximately 20–40 simultaneous students based on testing.

📱

One-Click Access

WHY NETLESS?

A Library That Travels Where the Internet Cannot.

Millions of learners face unreliable, unaffordable, or unavailable Internet access. Netless Library brings educational resources directly to learners through a compact, portable offline device.

📡

No Internet Required

Creates its own local Wi-Fi network for easy offline access.

📚

Educational Content

Access learning resources, documents, and digital content offline.

📱

Any Device

🌱

Ultra-Low Cost

Designed to make educational access affordable and accessible.
