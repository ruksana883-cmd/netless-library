The idea

Big knowledge, built from
almost nothing.

Most offline-library projects start with a computer and strip things down. Netless started with two parts and asked how much learning they could carry.

Ultra-minimal hardware

Just an ESP32 microcontroller and a 256 GB microSD card — no Linux computer, no server, no external router.

Genuinely offline

Netless creates its own local Wi-Fi network. No internet connection or mobile data ever enters the picture.

Works with what students have

Any phone, tablet, or laptop connects through a standard browser — no app to install, nothing to learn.

Ultra-compact, fits in a palm
~30 m range, 20–40 students at once
No Linux install, ever
Online in one tap via QR code

How it works

Learning in three simple steps.

1

Power on

Connect the compact Netless device to any USB power source — a wall adapter or power bank.

2

Connect

Students join the Netless Wi-Fi network from their own phone, tablet, or laptop — no password hassle.

3

Learn

Open a standard browser and the full offline library loads instantly. That's the whole process.
